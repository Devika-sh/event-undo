CI

Catalyst IEDC

Innovation & Entrepreneurship

Catalyst is the Innovation and Entrepreneurship Development Cell at Mar Baselios College of Engineering and Technology. It exists to turn half-formed student ideas into things that ship — products, prototypes, and the occasional company.

Through Evening Cafe meetups, pitch nights, bootcamps and a year-round mentor network, Catalyst has backed more than sixty student teams and runs the campus pre-incubation programme with the Kerala Startup Mission.

µL

MuLearn MBCET

Peer Learning Community

MuLearn is a peer-to-peer learning community where students pick an interest group, set their own pace, and learn in the open. No lectures, no attendance registers — just a circle that keeps showing up for each other.

The campus chapter runs weekly interest-group sessions across web development, design, cybersecurity and AI, plus Learning Fests and hackathons that connect members to the wider MuLearn network across Kerala.

IEEE

IEEE SB MBCET

Technical Chapter

The IEEE Student Branch is the college's technical chapter, affiliated to the world's largest professional body for engineers. It runs the societies — Computer, Power & Energy, WIE — that give students somewhere specific to go deep.

Expect paper presentations, standards workshops, industrial visits and the annual technical symposium, alongside volunteering opportunities that carry across every IEEE section in the country.
